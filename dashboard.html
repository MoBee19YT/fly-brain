How to read this. Each frame (60 per second) the game state comes in, the fly's eyes turn it into the firing of 32 groups of real visual neurons (LC4, LPLC2… the looming and object detectors flies use to dodge a swatter). That activity spreads through the real FlyWire wiring of 1,060 central and descending neurons. A trained readout of the 160 descending neurons, the cells that carry commands from brain to body, decides hold or release. The wiring itself was never changed; only the eye inputs and that readout were learned.
